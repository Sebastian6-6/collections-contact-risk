# collections-contact-risk

## Purpose
Technical data case "Contato Seguro em Cobrança" (debt-collection data treatment). Ingest debtor records from three providers (alfa, beta, gama: historical load up to 2026-08-31 plus September/2026 daily deliveries) and the September contact-attempt log, model them through bronze/silver/gold into a single trusted debtor registry, and build a contact-risk scale per phone and e-mail validated against contact history, with CNPJ enrichment from Receita Federal open data. The anonymized brief is `.context/raw/case-tecnico-dados-cobranca.md` (converted copy in `.context/converted/`); repository rules are in `AGENTS.md`.
